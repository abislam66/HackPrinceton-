# Color

The full chapter is 03 Color in the guidelines PDF and `01 Brand Guidelines/Chapter Sheets/color-sheet.png`.
`tokens.css` and `tokens.json` hold every value. They are generated from the deck by `node QC/sync.mjs`, so never edit them by hand.

Cone `#FF2800` is the accent, on whatever should be seen first. The old orange `#FF4900` is retired.

Pages sit on Plaster `#F4EDE2`. Asphalt `#0B0908` and Cone are for chapter breaks and big moments. No pure white, no pure black.

Minerals are the house neutrals (warm). Materials are true neutrals, an alternate for use over photos or a client's colors. Metals are cool, for screens, data and technical drawings.

Molten is the heat range: one band per page at most, never behind body copy. Brights support Cone in charts, signals and icons and are too loud to fill a page. Status red and green mean pass and fail only, never decoration.

Metals and Cast render as one clean ramp, deep to bright. No faux metallic sheen, bevels or highlight streaks.

Contrast: body text 4.5 : 1, large type (24 px and up) 3 : 1. Cone on Plaster is 3.25 : 1, so Cone text is large type only.

Names the deck marks as proposed are still proposed.
