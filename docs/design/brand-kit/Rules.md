# Rules from Michael

Standing rules he has given across the project that apply to any Tradesmark work. The note behind each one, with the date and his words, is in `Source Notes/`. His newest instruction wins when two disagree.

## Composition and spacing

Composition is judged before anything else. Correct motion or copy on a badly composed layout earns nothing.

Text keeps one gutter from any rule, border or hairline. Readouts and labels never touch a line. Vertical rules align across sections.

Pin content to the top and edge of its area and stack the rest under it. Center only a lone item.

Labels need real air from the content beside them. Keep generous gutters on every page.

No redundant eyebrows: never a label that repeats the heading under it.

## Color, surface and image

No faux metallic. No texture of any kind (grain, halation, weave, bloom, paper) until he approves one; every round so far was rejected.

One relatively uniform grade. Almost never a colorizer, duotone or tint as a default.

Renders of devices sit in a void: no floor, wall or ground.

Line drawings follow his picks: Teenage Engineering manual style, isometric with a heavy outer contour and thin interior, flat gray, no accent, light ground by default. The rejected wireframe studies (grid, plate, technical, dots, outline, contour, lattice, hatching, mesh) never come back.

Marks drawn on photos are expert craft: one Cone hairline, numbers on the image, words off it, the fault marked where it is with the fix beside it.

Inspiration comes from brand design studios (Pentagram, Kurppa Hosk, Koto, Collins, DixonBaxi), not corporate guideline portals.

## Motion and video

Slow means tempo, not slow motion. Long sweeps broken by bursts of 8 to 10 frame cuts. No fade-outs; end on a hard cut. No text shadows.

Logo builds never show a seam, double edge, overlap or wrong shape. One masked path per part. Zoom-check every build frame at 2x over the real background.

Every export has a human name. 30 fps is the default with 60 alongside, every video also comes in native 4K, BT.709 tagged.

Run the full motion-design-review gate on every render before he sees it. No exceptions for quick fixes.

## Copy

Short and plain, said the way a person on the team would say it. One or two lines per page. Cut before adding.

Titles say what the thing is. No slogan headlines. Titles use "&", body copy keeps "and". American spelling.

No AI structure: no invented frameworks, no counts in titles, no bold-label lists, no "not X but Y", no rule of three, no em dashes.

Captions carry a fact the photo cannot show; describing the frame is a photo-log entry, not copy.

Never invent a statistic, client, turnaround or capability. Facts come from `08 Company Facts/Company Profile.md`.

Explainer copy follows the `tradesmark-spec-copy` skill. He writes direction and ideology; his edits win.

## Checking

Check visual work in WebKit, Chromium and Firefox. Any canvas or shader layer gets a CSS opacity ceiling and a kill switch if it renders wrong.

One edit at a time with his eye on it. No batches of variants unless he asks.
